How to run a worker Read the protocol

Fair questions.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. If you can open an app and type, you can use it. There's nothing to set up and nothing to configure.

Isn't this the same as ChatGPT or OpenRouter?

The apps look similar; underneath they're opposites. Those are one company taking your request through their own computers. rootmode has no company in the middle — your app talks straight to whoever is answering.

Why is it cheaper?

Nobody's paying for enormous data centres. People use computers they already own, and they compete with each other on price. The app itself is free.

Who can see what I type?

Only the computer answering it, and it isn't kept afterwards. If you'd rather keep everything at home, you can run it entirely on your own machine.

What's the catch?

It depends on people running workers. The more machines on the network, the faster and cheaper it gets for everyone.

What if you disappear?

It keeps working. There's no central service to switch off — it's people's computers helping each other, and that carries on without us.
